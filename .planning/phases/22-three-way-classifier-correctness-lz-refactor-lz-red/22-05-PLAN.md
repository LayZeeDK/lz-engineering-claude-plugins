<objective>
Build the content-independent half of the placement A/B instrument: the three-arm plumbing in the
existing driver, the routing eval suite derived from the one tracked corpus, and the
destination-extraction grader with its offline fixtures. Nothing here depends on the final wording of
the classifier, so it runs in parallel with the content wave. Nothing here spends a token.

Purpose: D-11 requires an arm toggle over three plugin TREES, and the only script with `--plugin-dir`
arm composition is the lz-refactor driver -- Phase 20's subagent fan-out cannot present three trees at
all. The smallest correct build is six additive edits, not a new harness. And D-12 requires the metric
to be a DESTINATION label rather than a phrase set, because Phase 20's phrase-set grader measured house
vocabulary and inflated its headline roughly threefold.

Output: `run-e2e.mjs` with two new consts and one new arm-group token; `tabulate-mechanical-red.mjs`
with a grade-file parameter; the `e2e-rgr-routing` suite (suite, targets, nine prompts); and
`grade-routing.mjs` with fixtures and a GREEN `--selfcheck`. The variant trees, their builder and the
composition selfcheck are 22-06's; the RUN-GATE and the HALT are 22-07's.

## Locked decisions honored
- **D-08:** Three placement variants are measured, not argued: A inline only, B shared table plus inline rationale, C shared table replacing the narrative sections. This plan builds the arms that will carry them.
- **D-11:** A trigger eval cannot discriminate the arms -- their descriptions are byte-identical by construction. The instrument is a body/behavior eval with the arms toggled via `--plugin-dir`, using the generated-tree toggle pattern the driver already carries twice.
- **D-12:** The metric is routing correctness per request, graded on the DESTINATION and never on house vocabulary. The corpus is the nine cases.
- **D-13:** Two-stage design. Stage 1 screens at low k across three arms; stage 2 escalates only on a signal and only on the discriminating subset. Report Pass@k and Pass^k. The stage structure is DOCUMENTED here and gated in 22-07.
- **D-14:** Build the harness and prove every deterministic selfcheck GREEN first, then stop and ask. No metered command runs in this plan.
- **D-22:** The suite is `e2e-rgr-routing`, deliberately outside the auto-discovered namespace. `selfcheck-red.mjs` stays byte-unchanged; its sibling `selfcheck-routing.mjs` arrives in 22-06.
- **D-23:** The prompts derive from the tracked corpus, whose header already declares which cases are RECONSTRUCTED. Do not re-state provenance in nine places; cite the corpus.
