## Why tpp-recall is outside (measured from traces)

Per case: 9 cases fired 3 of 3, trigger-lz-tpp-02 fired 2 of 3, and trigger-lz-tpp-03, -05 and -07 fired 0 of 3.
Of the 10 failed runs, 5 ended at `max_turns` 3 having called only Grep/Glob on the empty run folder, 4 tried Bash
(not in the case's tools) after recon, and 1 answered in text. The recorded run used the vendored skill-creator
`run_eval.py`: no turn cap, the repository root as working folder, and recon calls ignored until the model chose a
skill or another tool. Under that detector the 5 cut-off runs are undecided, not misses. Inferred, not measured: with
a higher turn cap, some of those runs would have fired. The trigger cases' `max_turns: 3` came from the lz-oop
analog in Plan 03.
